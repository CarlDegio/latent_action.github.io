Metric reference

Formulas · Interpretation · Direction

A = oₜ, B = oₜ₊₁, R = recon. The observations oₜ and oₜ₊₁ are consecutive frames; recon is the reconstruction. S, E, and P denote SSIM, MSE, and PSNR. Base formulas are listed below.

Calculation methods

Success rate

For each experiment and head, success rates are averaged with equal weights: 4 components for LIBERO / LIBERO-Plus and 2 for RoboTwin. The DAP/LAP mean equally weights the two head means for the same experiment. Original zeros are retained, and different experiments are not aggregated. If either head is missing, their combined mean is also missing.

s = (s₁ + … + sₖ) / k
s_DAP/LAP = (s_Head1 + s_Head2) / 2

Pearson and Spearman

Pearson measures linear association. Spearman measures monotonic association by ranking each variable, assigning average ranks to ties, and computing Pearson correlation between the ranks.

r = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)(yᵢ − ȳ) / √[Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² Σ(yᵢ − ȳ)²]
ρ = Pearson(rank(x), rank(y))

Linear regression

The proxy metric is x and mean success rate (0–1) is y. Ordinary least squares includes an intercept. The chart displays success rates as percentages; the fitted equation uses the original 0–1 scale.

ŷ = β₀ + β₁x
β₁ = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)(yᵢ − ȳ) / Σ(xᵢ − x̄)²
β₀ = ȳ − β₁x̄

Missing values, constants, and interpretation

Each metric uses only experiments with valid values for both the metric and success rate, so n may vary. Correlations are undefined with fewer than 2 valid pairs or a constant variable. Interpret small samples cautiously; correlation does not imply causation.

Mean ratio / Gain follows the row-level formulas in the source worksheet; these values are not necessarily averages of per-sample ratios or gains. Metrics missing from the source remain missing.
